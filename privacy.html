Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

In short: your audio files never leave your device. Audio Trimmer has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no cookies. We don't collect, sell or share personal data.

1. Who we are

Audio Trimmer ("we", "us") is a free web tool for trimming and cutting audio, available at https://audiotrimmer.hirak-das.workers.dev/. For anything about this policy, message us on Telegram: @hirakhax on Telegram.

2. Your audio files

When you open a file, your browser reads it from your device and decodes, plays, edits and exports it locally using the Web Audio API. The file and the exported result are never sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see, store, access or recover your files. Closing or reloading the page discards everything held in the page's memory.

3. Information we collect

We do not collect personal data through the tool. We have no sign-up, no contact forms, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts.

Server logs. Like every website, the hosting provider that delivers these pages may automatically record technical data for each request, such as IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. These logs are used only to deliver the site, keep it secure and prevent abuse, and are kept for a short period by the host. We do not combine them with other data or use them to identify you.

4. Legal bases (EEA, UK and similar laws)

Where data protection laws such as the EU GDPR or UK GDPR apply, the limited server log processing described above is based on our legitimate interest in operating a secure, working website (Article 6(1)(f)).

5. Cookies and local storage

We use no cookies and no tracking. If you choose light or dark mode, that choice (just the word "light" or "dark") is saved in your browser's local storage so the page remembers it. It never leaves your device. See our Cookie Policy.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell or share personal information, including for cross-context behavioural advertising as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA). We do not process sensitive personal information.

7. International transfers

Your audio stays on your device, so it is never transferred. Server logs may be processed by our hosting provider in the countries where it operates, under the provider's own safeguards.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. These include rights under the GDPR and UK GDPR (Europe and the UK), the CCPA/CPRA and other US state privacy laws, Brazil's LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA, India's DPDP Act, Australia's Privacy Act, Japan's APPI, South Africa's POPIA and similar laws. Because we hold no personal data beyond short-lived server logs, there is usually nothing for us to provide, but you can contact us any time and we will reply within the time required by law. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

The tool is suitable for general audiences and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

10. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Because your files are processed on your own device, the main protection for them is the security of your device and browser.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the site handles data (for example by adding analytics), we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.

12. Contact

Message @hirakhax on Telegram.
