How to trim audio in 4 steps

  1. Drop your file

    Drop an audio file on the box above, or click it to choose one. Nothing is uploaded.

  2. Select the parts

    Drag across the waveform to select a part. Drag again anywhere else to add more.

  3. Save as a new timeline

    Click Save. The selected parts are joined into a new timeline below, ready to play.

  4. Export

    Click Export on the timeline you want. It downloads as a WAV file.

Trim audio online without uploading it

Most online audio cutters send your file to a server, process it there and send it back. Audio Trimmer doesn't. The page uses your browser's built-in audio engine to open, play and export the file on your own computer or phone. Nothing is uploaded, so it's fast even with big files and safe for private recordings, interviews and voice notes.

Features

  • Multi-part cutting: pick several parts of a song or recording and join them into one clip.
  • Instant preview: edits are stored as timestamps, so playback is instant and nothing is re-encoded until you export.
  • Every timeline is kept: each Save makes a new timeline, so you can compare versions and export any of them.
  • Unlimited undo and redo for every cut, save and delete.
  • Clean joins: a tiny fade at each join removes clicks and pops.
  • Zoom on each timeline for cuts accurate to the millisecond.
  • Works with video files too: open an MP4 or WebM to trim its sound.

Frequently asked questions

Are my audio files uploaded to a server?

No. The file is opened and edited entirely inside your browser. Nothing is sent over the internet, so it works for private recordings too.

Is Audio Trimmer free?

Yes. It's free to use with no sign-up, no watermark and no file size limit other than your device's memory.

Which audio formats can I open?

Any format your browser can play: usually MP3, WAV, M4A/AAC, OGG, OPUS and FLAC. You can also open a video file to trim its sound.

What format does it export?

Timelines are exported as uncompressed 16-bit WAV at the original sample rate, which every editor and player can open.

Can I cut out several parts and join them?

Yes. Select as many parts as you like, then click Save. They are joined in time order into a new timeline, with a tiny fade at each join so there are no clicks.

Does it change my original file?

No. Every edit is stored as a list of timestamps, so you can undo anything. Only the exported file contains the cut audio.
