Cookie Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

In short: Audio Trimmer uses no cookies and no tracking. If you pick light or dark mode, that one choice is saved in your browser so the page remembers it. That's all, so there's no cookie banner.

What we use

TechnologyUsed?Details
Cookies (first or third party)NoNo cookies are set by this site.
Local storageOnly if you choose a themeOne item named theme with the value light or dark, saved when you pick Light or Dark in the top-right switch. It stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is removed when you pick System.
Session storage, IndexedDBNoYour audio and edits live only in the page's memory and are gone when you close or reload it.
Analytics, ads, tracking pixels, fingerprintingNoThere are no third-party scripts on the site.

Consent

Laws such as the EU ePrivacy Directive and the GDPR require consent for non-essential cookies and similar storage. The theme item is stored only when you ask for it, to provide the feature you requested, which these laws treat as strictly necessary. There is nothing else to consent to, so no banner is shown.

Your browser

Your browser may cache the site's files (HTML, images) so it loads faster and works offline. This is normal browser behaviour, stores no personal data, and you can clear it in your browser settings at any time.

Changes

If we ever add cookies or similar technologies, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
